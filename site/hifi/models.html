From demand to a day-ahead price

gridflow-models is a separate Python library that reads gridflow's silver tables. It forecasts GB demand, wind and solar, builds the GB supply stack, and clears demand against that stack for a day-ahead price.

It produces forecasts and prices for research.

  • Day-ahead demand

    Forecasts GB national demand for every half-hour of the next day, as a spread of quantiles in MW.

    Validated: median pinball 711.04 MW, 90% coverage 0.893 (version 1, 1 September 2024 to 22 August 2026).

    Version 2 adds weather from open_meteo/historical_demand.

    day_ahead.lgbm_demand.v1 day_ahead.lgbm_demand.v2

  • Wind generation

    Forecasts GB metered wind output for every half-hour of the next day, from weather at twelve wind farm sites.

    Weather from open_meteo/historical_wind.

    wind.lgbm_quantile.v1

  • Solar generation

    Forecasts GB embedded solar output for every half-hour of the next day, from weather at six solar sites.

    Weather from open_meteo/historical_solar.

    solar.lgbm_quantile.v1

  • Residual demand

    Demand less wind and solar is residual demand.

    The published price run uses recorded demand, wind and solar rather than the forecasts, so it gives one price per half-hour.

  • GB merit-order stack

    Builds the GB supply curve for one half-hour: available plant ranked by short-run marginal cost, in cumulative MW.

    Constructive: it is rebuilt for each half-hour and has no parameters to fit.

    Fuel and carbon prices are synthetic.

    stack.gb.v1

  • Fundamentals SMP

    Clears residual demand against the stack every half-hour for a GB day-ahead price, scored against Elexon's market index.

    It has nothing to fit.

    fundamentals_smp.gb.v1
