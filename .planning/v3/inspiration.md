## ref-01 — "Poweris" community-energy landing page (dropped 2026-08-02, screenshot in chat)

**What it is:** Consumer landing page for a community renewable-energy product. Full-bleed
flat-vector illustrated hero: teal/petrol sky, city silhouette behind layered hills, four
white wind turbines, solar-roofed houses in a chartreuse/olive field, dark-forest foliage
+ wildflowers in the foreground. White rounded geometric sans headline ("Start energy
revolution from your community"), navy pill CTA, leaf logomark.

**User's read:** likes it; wants "embracing electrification + onboarding of renewables as
how power systems develop" as a theme element — a renewable-energy / good-for-environment
vibe, **but explicitly NOT hippie / not too much pro-environment stuff**. "An element we
feed in," not the whole identity.

**Take (transfers well to a data-docs portfolio):**
- **Palette family** — the strongest lift. Layered-landscape palette: deep petrol/teal,
  a green spectrum (olive → chartreuse → deep forest), warm off-white, navy ink. A real
  alternative to the cream+terracotta AI-default, and it's subject-grounded (sky, land,
  grid) rather than eco-cliché.
- **Layered depth** — background→foreground silhouette bands (sky / hills / field /
  foliage) as a compositional device: could become section dividers, chart backdrops, or
  a crafted inline-SVG hero band. Pairs naturally with the existing deterministic-SVG
  chart system.
- **Optimism with engineering artifacts** — turbines, pylons, rooftop solar, transmission
  lines, city load centers. The "energy transition as infrastructure" story — which is
  literally what the site's datasets document (FUELHH generation mix, wind/solar
  forecasts, NESO carbon intensity, interconnector flows).

**Leave (fights the site's job / the anti-goals):**
- The overall **SaaS-landing shape** (product hero + CTA + Plans/FAQ nav) — site anti-goal
  ("not a product"). This ref is a marketing page; ours is documentation.
- **Eco-clichés** — leaf logomark, wildflowers, "revolution" copyvoice. User's "not
  hippie" constraint → do-not-use list: leaves, globes, hands, nature-first framing.
  Optimism comes from the engineered grid, not from foliage.
- The **rounded consumer sans** as-is — friendly but credibility-light for an
  energy-markets data audience; if a geometric/humanist sans enters, it needs a technical
  counterweight (mono for data).

**Direction seed this opens:** "**The electrified landscape**" — illustrated/layered
energy-transition warmth fused with editorial documentation structure. Signature-element
candidates: a crafted SVG grid-landscape hero band; generation-mix palette used as the
site's categorical color system; a stylized decarbonization/demand curve as a recurring
motif.
