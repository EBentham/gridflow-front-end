parsed from text, grouped by `auction_product` (PBR petrol, NBR clay), aggregation `mean` of `clearing_price`

between 2 and 10. NBR at most 1.54 (25 May 2024), £0 on every day from 4 December 2024. Both products fit
because a line takes several series.

**Date convention (stated in the caption and x label):** the window text is read as UTC and each window goes
to the UK day running 23:00 to 23:00 (NESO's companion package describes delivery "from 23:00 to 23:00"). On
real silver this puts every one of the 596 auctions on exactly one day and no day gets two auctions. NESO
states no clock for these strings; the measured clock-change pattern (in the note body) supports UTC.

**Python change (authorised by the seat's note "derive a date in distil from the text"):** a new opt-in spec
key `time_text: {format, day: {zone, starts}}`. Specs without it behave exactly as before; no other page's
spec or digest changes. `build.py` and the template are untouched.
- `src/gridflow_front_end/chart_spec.py`: key allowed, documented, validated (`_check_time_text`); a bar chart
  rejects it like `time`.
- `src/gridflow_front_end/distil.py`: `_time_from_text` (parse with `str.to_datetime`, then optionally UTC to
  the zone inside polars, shift by `-starts` hours, truncate to the day); `_scan` uses it when the key is
  present.
- Tests: `tests/test_distil.py::test_text_times_go_to_the_uk_day_that_starts_at_2300` (GMT and BST edges)
  and three invalid cases in `tests/test_chart_spec.py`. `pytest -x -q tests/test_chart_spec.py
  tests/test_distil.py`: 45 passed.

The earlier one-week bar chart (19 to 25 January 2025) is replaced; the notebook still shows that week's
