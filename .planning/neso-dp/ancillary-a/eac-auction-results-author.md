## What the three shapes are (plainly)

Different files, not overlapping copies, with one exception:

- **Current** (`eac_results_summary`, `_by_unit`, `_buy_orders`): one CKAN datastore dump each, delivery from the
  1 April 2026 day (`2026-03-31T22:00:00`). Measured on the second capture: the dump **grows by appending**; every
  earlier row is kept with identical values.
- **FY archives** (`_summary_fy`, `_by_unit_fy`, `_buy_orders_fy`): three uploaded files each, NESO's FY2023
  (from `2023-11-02T23:00:00`), FY2024 and FY2025, ending `2026-03-31T21:30:00`. They share no key and no auction
  with the current tables and join them without a gap: summary FY plus current is 1,080 auctions on 1,080
  consecutive delivery days, 3 November 2023 to 17 October 2026, none on two days.
- **Daily** (`eac_results_daily_*`): the latest auction only (NESO's text), **replaced** each day; its rows repeat the
  current table's. This is the one overlap: never add a daily file to its current table.
- **Sell orders** have no FY member: `eac_results_sell_orders` is one family of the current-month dump (from the
  1 October day) plus 35 monthly archives `202311` to `202609`, key-disjoint. Times are raw strings (one archive,
  `202607`, uses a space separator). `eac_results_daily_sell_orders` has typed times.

## The chart

Line chart, silver `neso_data_portal/eac_results_summary`, `clearing_price` (£/MW/h), mean per delivery day, four
response products (DRL clay, DCL petrol, DCH horizon, DRH olive), window 1 April to 17 October 2026, 200 days x 4
series, 4,800 rows used, no gaps.

- **Day convention.** Distil can only bucket a typed datetime by UTC day, which would put each day's first EFA block
  (22:00Z in BST, 23:00 UK) on the day before. The spec uses `time: delivery_end` with `time_bucket: 1d`: the end of
  every four-hour block falls inside its delivery day's UTC date. Checked on all 38,880 response rows of the FY and
  current summaries: the UTC date of `delivery_end` equals the 23:00-to-23:00 UK day of the window on every row,
  clock-change days included. No Python change.
- **Two captures.** Silver now holds the 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-16 captures of the current dump (see Unverified and
  Body corrections), so the spec and the sample both carry `dedup` on the key, newest capture last
  (`duplicates_dropped` 4,584). The rows dropped are identical copies.
- **Why four products, not six.** The six response products sit in bands (daily medians DRL 17.14, DML 7.57, DCL 2.94,
  DCH 2.14, DMH -0.24, DRH -10.58), but the brief allows price lines only petrol, horizon, olive and clay, plus one
  dashed series; clay and bronze are near-identical. Containment (largest MW: a mean of about 1,340 to 1,360 MW per window in the current summary) and Regulation
  (the signed extreme) are drawn; the caption says Moderation is in the notebook, which plots all six monthly from
  November 2023. If Bobbo prefers all six, the spec only needs DML and DMH back plus a bronze and a dashed key entry.
- **Values** (committed series): DRL 6.06 (5 April) to 38.067 (24 June), median 17.14; DCL 1.077 (3 May) to 12.523
  (22 September); DCH 0.045 (28 April) to 7.202 (24 June); DRH -16.52 (16 October) to 7.053 (5 April), below zero on
  190 of 200 days. These match an independent Polars computation (`eac4.py` in the scratchpad) to the third decimal.

## Template problems

1. **Four requests per member is too few for sell orders.** `eac_results_sell_orders` reads 36 files (one dump, 35
   monthly archives); `MEMBER_REQUESTS_MAX = 4` (`page_fields.py:207`). The page shows the dump, the first and the
   last archive, and `differs` says "35 monthly archives". A member-level "N more files like this" line, or a pattern
   request, would be more honest.
2. **No day bucket for typed datetimes that starts at 23:00 UK.** `time_text.day` only applies to text columns
   (`distil.py:142-161, 185-186`). Worked around with `time: delivery_end`, exact for four-hour blocks only; a half-hour
   reserve chart could not use the trick (its first window ends at 23:30 UK, the evening before). A `day` option on
   typed datetimes would cover every EAC product.
3. **Price-line paints.** Only four paints are allowed for price lines, which caps a price chart at five series.

## Defects

- **gridflow_models (refines remediation 20c, EAC BR):** for the ten `utc_instant` families of `eac-auction-results`,
  `query()` filters `timestamp_utc` = window start in UTC by **UTC date**, so a date window cuts each 23:00-to-23:00 UK
  delivery day: the first EFA block (22:00Z in BST, 23:00Z in GMT) lands on the previous date. Users must filter on
  `auction_id` or use `data.sql`. For `eac_results_sell_orders` (temporal `none`) `query()` filters on the capture time,
  as on EAC BR, so a date window returns all rows or none. Evidence: `_DATE_COLUMN_BY_DATASET` gives `timestamp_utc`
  for all eleven; `_date_range_predicate` in `_get_method_registry.py:62-96`; registry temporal kinds.
- **gridflow registry, sell-order times (carried, ruling 738):** the three time columns of `eac_results_sell_orders`
  are raw strings in two layouts (`T` and space, archive `202607`), so the 33.5M sell rows have no typed delivery time
  and `timestamp_utc` is the capture time. A per-resource format map for datetime columns would type them.
- **gridflow registry, download cap (carried from the note):** `max_download_bytes` 536,870,912; the newest monthly sell
  archives are 407 to 438 MB, so a month larger than 512 MiB would fail capture.
- **Vendor dictionary (carried):** `auctionProduct` lists ten codes but the bodies carry `NBR` and `PBR` too; no code is
  expanded; `status` lists `EXCLUDED` (absent) and omits `PARTIALLY_EXECUTED` (present).
