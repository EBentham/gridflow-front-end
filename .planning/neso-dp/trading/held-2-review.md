## 1. `historic-gtma-grid-trade-master-agreement-trades-data` (leads `gtma_trades_data`, `gtma_trades_data_pre`, `gtma_trades_fy`) - APPROVE

The two eligible-table lines are true, plain and do not misstate the ledger: neither claims the table is held on the unit question, and the ledger's only open item for the main table (the 2020-21 repeats, resource-level) is exactly the second half of the first line. Members, lead and order match the registry and manifest.

1. **nit** `page.what_it_is`, `page.family.members[0].differs`: "give a trade's total MWh" and "total MWh per trade" state the unit as fact, while the `Rows not shown` line beside them says NESO's package text says MW. NESO's dictionary does say MWh (and, measured, the rows agree), but the page cannot cite that, so attribute it. Suggest `what_it_is`: "Files to June 2021 give a trade's total, which NESO's dictionary calls MWh;" and `differs`: "Six yearly files, 2015-16 to 2020-21 (first and newest shown); total per trade, MWh". Check budgets.
2. **nit** `page.what_it_is`: "NESO warns that delivered trades can change, for example if a counterparty defaults". NESO's words are "post-delivery things can change, for example counterparties can default (not deliver the trade)". A defaulting counterparty means the trade was not delivered. Suggest "NESO warns that trades can change after delivery, for example if a counterparty defaults, and the data may not be updated."
3. **nit** `page.family.members[0].held`: "the 2020-21 file repeats rows" is right but the reader cannot tell how much; "repeats ten trades" would be more exact if the budget allows (15 words now). The dictionary this line cites is the 2020-21 one; the 2015-16 to 2019-20 dictionaries are not saved here (the writer's unverified item). "its dictionary" is as the note words it; fine.
4. **nit** `page.family.members[2].held`: reuses the approved `Cost` wording unchanged, which is correct and consistent. The held-1 review's suggestion (state the clash: a rate in £/h against a total in £) is still open on the approved pages; if the seat adopts it, apply it here too so all four pages match.

Checked and fine: `facts` (seven uploads, six datastore tables; monthly and morning refresh as NESO's words; UTC start and end; April 2015; tables to 2026-27); `summary`; `how_used` ("system trades against energy trades" matches the `SO Flag` and `SO_Flag` definitions); the three commands (keys exist; `--last 24h` as on approved pages); every member `differs` (pre-June file: same seven columns, MWh, £/MWh, £; FY: average MW, `Reason` and `Note` only in FY); related links (upcoming trades, enactment, interconnector auctions each supported by NESO text); `page.title` and `facts.vendor` carry both names.

## 2. `interconnector-requirement-and-auction-summary-data` (lead `ic_requirement_auction`) - APPROVE

The auction line is true as NESO's dump behaves: the 8 October lots `2026_10_08-11_43-1` and `-2` were blank then, and by 16 October the same rows carried results. "The file keeps no earlier state" is right: both captures hold one row per lot id and the changed rows keep no trace of the old values.

1. **nit** `page.held`: "NESO updates each lot in place" reads as every lot. NESO says a lot "will be updated" when its auction concludes, "the requirement may not be completely filled", and 230 lots in the 16 October body still have no clearing price. Suggest "a lot": "NESO updates a lot in place with its auction result; the file keeps no earlier state." (same 16 words). The line also leaves out the consequence that is the actual ledger question (what was known when); optional: "NESO overwrites each lot with its auction result, so what was known when cannot be told." (16 words).
2. **nit** `page.related[1]` (`elexon/soso`): "System operators' own trades across GB interconnectors, from Elexon" says what that page is, not how it relates. The link is reasonable (both are system-operator interconnector trading); suggest "Elexon's system operator to system operator interconnector trades" or leave.
3. **nit** `page.facts.vendor`: the other two pages name NESO's package; this one does not ("NESO Data Portal: one datastore table and a market notice PDF"). Suggest "NESO Data Portal, package Interconnector Requirement and Auction Summary Data: one datastore table and a PDF", if the budget allows. The page title also drops "Data" from NESO's package title.

Checked and fine: `what_it_is` against NESO's notes (six interconnectors, constraints or margin, qualified counterparties need a GTMA, requirements not firm, volumes and prices added when the auction ends); `grain`; cadence (registry refresh is ad hoc as well); the three requests equal the sidecars; third command key `ic_requirement_auction_files` exists (`kind: files`, PDF bronze only); no `history` fact and none invented; related GTMA note ("every bidder here must hold a GTMA") is NESO's "required to have a GTMA in place".

## 3. `upcoming-trades` (lead `upcoming_trades`) - REVISE

1. **major** `page.held`: "NESO labels `Volume` MWh but multiplies it by duration for cost" states as NESO's action what is only a comment in NESO's dictionary ("Cost = Price * Volume * Duration"), and the rows do not follow it. In every row of both captures (28 on 8 October, 48 on 16 October) `Cost` equals `Volume` x `Price` exactly, with no duration factor (for example SP 47: 110 x 218 = 23,980; SP 29: -3.5 x 76.96 = -269.36), although each row is one half-hour period. The approved enactment pages say NESO "describes" or "defines" the formula; this line says it "multiplies". Say what the dictionary says, attributed, within 16 words, for example: "NESO's cost note multiplies `Volume`, labelled MWh, by duration, and `Last_Updated` has no clock." (14 words). The `Last_Updated` half is right: unit "N/A", no zone in the dictionary; the same wording is approved for the older balancing table.
2. **nit** `page.related[0].note`: "Delivered GTMA trades" is backed by NESO trading material (FACTS 3.9) but the GTMA page itself, quoting NESO, says defaulting counterparties mean some trades are not delivered. Suggest "Past GTMA trades; this table lists those still to come" (10 words).
3. **nit** `page.raw_feed.note`: "Each capture is the whole list as it stood." is right (`latest: whole_capture`) but "capture" is gridflow's word and "as it stood" has no time. Optional: "Each fetch replaces the whole list."

Checked and fine: `summary`, `what_it_is` (forecast balancing requirements, minimum cost, GTMA trades due for delivery, provisional and correctable, not to be relied on), `how_used`, cadence (ten minutes, NESO's words), grain (key `(id, date, sp)`, unique in both captures), requests, commands, the two other related links.
