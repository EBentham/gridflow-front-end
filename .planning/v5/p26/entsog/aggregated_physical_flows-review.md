# entsog/aggregated_physical_flows: review

Checker: Sonnet 5.5 · high, 2026-10-07. Inputs: the canonical note (diffed against `origin/master` in the vault worktree), the mirror, the three artefacts, the built page, the writer's report. Sources re-derived: gridflow `connectors/entsog/{endpoints,client}.py`, `silver/entsog/{generic,datetime}.py`, local silver (`aggregated_physical_flows`, `physical_flows`, `aggregate_interconnections`), bronze (`aggregated_physical_flows`, `physical_flows`).

## Verdict: REVISE

Three majors, six nits. No blocker. The data and the chart are right; the problems are what the page leaves the reader to assume about the zone, two unscoped "return no rows" statements, and a wrong count in the vault body.

## Findings

1. **major** · `page.title`, `page.summary`, `page.chart_view.title`, `page.chart_view.caption`, `page.what_it_is` · The page never says plainly what the table lacks, and its titles read as the zone's whole entry.
   - "Physical gas flows by zone" and "British zone entry by source" present three aggregates as entry by source. The only loss stated is "five asked, three return rows" (the Northern Ireland pair) and, in one key note, "Storage exit is not requested". Nothing says that entry by pipeline from other systems, every exit (distribution, final consumers, storage, neighbouring zones) and every cross-border aggregate are absent. So the table cannot give a zone balance, net storage, imports or demand. The author's report concedes the page "says only that storage exit is not requested; the vault body lists the rest", and the vault body (Overview, Known issues) already says "this is not a zone balance". The page does not.
   - Evidence that the omission matters: bronze `physical_flows` for National Gas (`UK-TSO-0001`) entry on gas day 21 Sep: all entry points sum to 1,558.0 GWh/d, the points named by the three aggregates sum to 876.1. The rest is St. Fergus 559.2 and Easington 122.8, both typed by the vendor `pointType` as "Cross-Border Transmission IP between ExtEU and Non-EU (import)". On 13 Sep: 1,309.8 against 511.3 (St. Fergus 473.2, Easington 325.3). The largest single entry source in the point data is not on the chart. (Local numbers, for evidence only; do not put them on the page.)
   - The register (`aggregate_interconnections` silver) lists 17 British-zone aggregates; the connector requests 5 (`endpoints.py:48-54`). The 12 not requested: `UK-TSO-0001` entry `Transmission`, `Transmission` + IE, IE+NI, NL, IUK (5); exit `Distribution`, `Final Consumers`, `Storage`, `Transmission` + IE, IE+NI, NL, IUK (7).
   - Fix, in `what_it_is` and `chart_view.caption` (and consider `title`): say in domain terms that the table holds only entry from production, storage and LNG terminals; entry from other transmission systems and all exits are not requested, so it is not the zone's total entry or its balance. A title like "British zone entry from production, storage and LNG" would not overclaim.

2. **major** · `page.what_it_is`, `page.raw_feed.note` · Unscoped "never returns" claims about the vendor, measured on a handful of fetches.
   - "three return rows" and "the two GNI (UK) filters for Northern Ireland return no rows" are written as how ENTSOG behaves. The evidence is 14 daily responses (`meta.count` 3, `meta.total` 6 in every body; no `IE-TSO-0001` record in any). Both combinations are valid in the register (`6UKUK---------IE-TSO-0001entry/exitTransmissionUK-NI------`), so absence in these responses is not a vendor rule, and the cause (no flow, or not reported) is unknown. The batch ruling is to scope observations to the responses we hold.
   - Fix: scope it ("in the responses fetched for this window, ... returned no record"). Do not assert a cause. (Optional, taste: "GNI (UK)" is the vendor's operator label; "the Northern Ireland pair" would read plainer.)

3. **major** · Canonical note, Known issues, "Zero storage entry" (also the writer's report) · Wrong count.
   - The note says Storage `value` is 0 on "7 of 14 gas days in 2026-08/09". Silver has 6: 2026-08-01, 08-03, 09-15, 09-18, 09-19, 09-20 (`agg.filter(Storage & value == 0)` returns 6 dates). The other eight days are 24.2M, 129.6M, 93.5M, 134.1M, 72.0M, 23.0M, 16.0M and 378.8M kWh/d.
   - The report also says "All 16 named points exist in `physical_flows`"; there are 13 distinct names (Production 4, Storage 7, LNG 2). The claim itself holds (all 13 are present at 04:00 UTC on all 14 days, no duplicate point-day), only the count is wrong.
   - Fix: "6 of 14" in the note. It is the canonical vault fact and gets mirrored.

4. **nit** · Canonical note, Known issues, "Teesside under `Production`" (and the report's Defects list) · "Unexplained" is explained by the bronze.
   - `physical_flows` bronze (gas day 2026-09-21) gives `pointType` "Aggregated production point - TP" for `LNG-00007` Teesside, which is why it sits under `Production`. The `LNG-` prefix is only the key's prefix. Likewise `LNG-00053` Avonmouth LNG has `pointType` "Storage point", which is why it is named under `Storage`. Remove the Teesside defect from the backlog list, and add the `pointType` for both. (The "6 counted against 7 named" Avonmouth observation stands.)

5. **nit** · Canonical note, Known issues, "Not requested" bullet · The list is incomplete and loosely worded.
   - It omits `UK-TSO-0001` entry `Transmission` (and the entry cross-border forms), and says every cross-border aggregate "(Ireland, NI, ...)" is not requested while the two NI-only aggregates are requested. List the 12 exactly (see finding 1) and say "Ireland and Northern Ireland combined" for `IE|NI`.

6. **nit** · Live `entsog/physical_flows` note, `page.related` (not the author's file) · "Physical flow summed by balancing zone rather than by point" now overstates the new page: the aggregate covers three sources, not the zone. Seat to reword when it next touches that note, for example "Physical flow summed by adjacent system for the British zone".

7. **nit** · `page.notebook.needs` · "gas days 13 to 21 September 2026 of both tables" needs `physical_flows` ingested and transformed, but `raw_feed.commands` only fetch the aggregate. Either name `physical_flows` in the note ("... and the point table `physical_flows`") or leave as is if the seat accepts it. Windows and ends otherwise agree (`query()` ends included, 13 to 21).

8. **nit** · `page.record.fields` · Four lines mostly restate cell values the frame shows: `indicator` ("Physical Flow"), `data_set_label` ("Aggregates"), `direction_key` ("`entry` here"), `adjacent_systems_key` ("`Production` or `Storage` here"). The rubric asks that no line repeats the frame. They also carry the vendor field name, so this is a nit.

9. **nit** · `page.chart_view.caption` · Every value is flagged `Provisionnal` by the vendor (frame and guide say so), but nothing near the chart says the plotted values are provisional, which a trader would want. Optional one clause.
