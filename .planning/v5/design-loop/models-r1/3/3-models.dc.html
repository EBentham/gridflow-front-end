From demand to a day-ahead price

gridflow-models is a separate Python library that reads gridflow's silver tables. It forecasts GB demand, wind and solar, builds the GB supply stack, and clears demand against that stack for a day-ahead price.

It produces forecasts and prices for research. It does not produce orders, positions or P&L.

Read the model cards

How the models connect

Demand less wind and solar is residual demand; the price is where residual demand meets the supply stack.

ModelWhat it doesFed by

  1. Day-ahead demand

    Forecasts GB national demand for every half-hour of the next day, as a spread of quantiles in MW.

    • elexon/indo target
    • open_meteo/historical_demand weather, version 2

    Version 1, from stored walk-forward backtests, 1 September 2024 to 22 August 2026.

    711.04 MW

    Pinball loss at the median is half the mean absolute error of the median forecast, in MW. Coverage is the share of outturns inside the 5 to 95% band.

  2. Wind generation

    Forecasts GB metered wind output for every half-hour of the next day, from weather at twelve wind farm sites.

    • elexon/fuelhh target
    • open_meteo/historical_wind weather

  3. Solar generation

    Forecasts GB embedded solar output for every half-hour of the next day, from weather at six solar sites.

    • neso_data_portal/historic_generation_mix target
    • open_meteo/historical_solar weather

  4. GB merit-order stack

    Builds the GB supply curve for one half-hour: available plant ranked by short-run marginal cost, in cumulative MW. Constructive: it is rebuilt for each half-hour and has no parameters to fit.

    • elexon/bmunits_reference units
    • elexon/remit availability
    • elexon/fou2t14d derating

  5. Fundamentals SMP

    Clears residual demand against the stack every half-hour for a GB day-ahead price, scored against Elexon's market index. It has nothing to fit. Its published run clears recorded demand, wind and solar, so each half-hour gets one price, not a spread.

    • elexon/indo recorded demand
    • elexon/fuelhh recorded wind
    • neso_data_portal/historic_generation_mix recorded solar
    • stack.gb.v1 supply curve per half-hour
    • elexon/mid scored against
